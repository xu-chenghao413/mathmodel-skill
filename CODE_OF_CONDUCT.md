# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome
good-faith corrections, and keep technical discussions focused on reproducible
behavior. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, credential
sharing, and publication of private contest or manuscript material are not
acceptable.

## Applying the standard

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this
standard or the repository's license, provenance, and privacy rules. For urgent
or sensitive concerns, use the private contact method listed in the maintainer's
GitHub profile rather than posting confidential details in an issue.
